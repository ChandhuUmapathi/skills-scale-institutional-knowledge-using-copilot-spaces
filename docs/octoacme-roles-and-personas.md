# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Design Lead

### Role Summary
Design Leads shape the user experience and ensure product decisions are grounded in usability, accessibility, and clear customer value. They help translate product needs into intuitive, testable experiences.

### Responsibilities
- Define UX direction and design principles for the initiative
- Review flows, prototypes, and user-facing changes with product and engineering
- Ensure accessibility, usability, and consistency across features
- Partner with PM and engineering to evaluate trade-offs and resolve design risks

### Goals
- Improve product usability and customer satisfaction
- Align design work with product outcomes and business priorities
- Reduce rework caused by unclear UX expectations

### Typical Communication
- Product and design reviews
- Cross-functional planning and milestone check-ins
- Feedback loops with user research and support teams

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works closely with Product Managers to validate outcomes and prioritize user needs
- Collaborates with Developers to ensure viable implementation and design feasibility
- Supports QA by clarifying acceptance criteria and user-experience expectations

---

## Release Manager

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate release readiness, sequencing, and communication so launches are well planned and low risk.

### Responsibilities
- Own release readiness checks and deployment sequencing
- Coordinate stakeholder communication before and after launch
- Maintain rollback plans and release notes
- Track dependencies and risks that could affect launch timing

### Goals
- Deliver releases with clear owner accountability and predictable communication
- Minimize surprise issues and post-release disruption
- Align engineering, support, and stakeholder expectations

### Typical Communication
- Release planning meetings
- Deployment readiness checklists
- Post-release status updates and incident escalation

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers and QA to confirm deployment readiness
- Coordinates with Product Managers and stakeholders on launch timing and messaging
- Escalates deployment risks to PM or Sponsor when business impact is significant

---

## QA Lead

### Role Summary
QA Leads define the quality strategy for a project and ensure products meet acceptance criteria before release.

### Responsibilities
- Define testing strategy, quality gates, and validation approach
- Coordinate manual and automated testing across key user flows
- Monitor defects, regressions, and release quality signals
- Help align test coverage with business and technical risk

### Goals
- Reduce defects and increase confidence before release
- Validate that delivery matches user needs and product expectations
- Improve consistency of quality standards across iterations

### Typical Communication
- QA planning and review meetings
- Defect triage with engineering and product
- Release readiness and risk discussions with PM and stakeholders

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers on testing plans and regression coverage
- Partners with Product Managers to translate acceptance criteria into validation criteria
- Aligns with Release Manager on go/no-go decisions and launch readiness

---

## Customer / Support Liaison

### Role Summary
The Customer / Support Liaison represents customer feedback, support issues, and service-impact concerns so product work remains aligned with real-world usage.

### Responsibilities
- Gather recurring customer pain points and support trends
- Surface issues that may require product or process changes
- Help prioritize fixes that improve customer experience
- Communicate release impacts and known issues to support teams

### Goals
- Improve customer satisfaction and reduce avoidable friction
- Ensure product decisions account for support realities and customer feedback
- Create a more informed feedback loop between delivery and customer-facing teams

### Typical Communication
- Customer feedback reviews
- Support escalations and issue routing
- Release note and incident communication

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Provides product context to Product Managers and engineering teams
- Shares operational risk and customer-impact details with PMs and stakeholders
- Supports Release Manager and QA Lead by highlighting user-facing risks and known issues
